# Home imagery prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the fictional Interactive Store portfolio. All devices are conceptual and unbranded. Reference product images are in this directory; `home-final-concept.png` served as the hero composition reference.

## Current panoramic hero — `home-hero-panoramic.png`

This wide image replaces the original hero on the Home page. It is blended into the left-side copy with a CSS mask in `assets/css/home.css`.

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: photographic right side of a panoramic website hero, not a webpage screenshot. Input image 1 is the user's layout reference: match only the calm wide composition, object placement, pale studio light and soft blending mood; ignore its words, buttons, logo and device branding. Input images 2–4 define the three fictional unbranded products: graphite Arc 14 laptop, black Pulse over-ear headphones, charcoal Haven smart speaker. Create a new photorealistic horizontal 16:9 editorial desk photograph. Keep the leftmost third very pale and quiet for a CSS fade. Place the laptop open with the dark graphite back of its display at roughly 30–58% of the frame, its top at 12–18% height. Rest the black headphones naturally in the foreground around the middle, fully visible, with their bottom above 88% height. Place the compact charcoal fabric speaker near the right side, fully visible with a subtle blue ring and bottom above 88% height. A small softly blurred green plant at the far right. Keep all product edges safely inside the frame; no part of the headphones or speaker may touch the bottom. Warm white desk, pale gray-white background, diffused window light, subtle shadows, elegant premium technology photography. No UI, no words, no logos, no people, no watermark, no extra products, no manufacturer resemblance.
